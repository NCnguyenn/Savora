# Food Delivery Project - Savora

## 1. Project Overview
Savora is a comprehensive, web-based food delivery platform designed to seamlessly connect customers, restaurant owners, and delivery drivers. The system facilitates real-time tracking, secure payments, and streamlined order management, providing a unified and efficient experience for all participants in the food delivery ecosystem.

## 2. Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (Vanilla JS for dynamic interactions and DOM manipulation)
- **Backend:** PHP (Handling business logic, authentication, and API endpoints)
- **Database:** MySQL (Relational database for storing user data, menus, orders, and transactions)
- **Server Environment:** XAMPP (Apache web server and MySQL database server)

## 3. System Roles & Responsibilities
The platform is designed around four distinct user roles, each with specific permissions and features tailored to their needs.

### 3.1. Customer
Customers are the end-users who use the platform to order food.
- **Search & Explore:** Browse various restaurants, search for specific cuisines or dishes, and view detailed restaurant menus with prices and descriptions.
- **Order Placement:** Add items to the shopping cart, customize dish preferences (e.g., spice level, extra toppings), and securely place orders.
- **Payment Integration:** Complete transactions safely using multiple integrated payment methods (e.g., Credit/Debit Cards, Digital Wallets, Cash on Delivery).
- **Delivery Tracking:** Track the real-time GPS location of the assigned delivery driver and monitor the live status of the order (e.g., Preparing, Out for Delivery, Arrived).
- **Profile Management:** Manage personal information, delivery addresses, view order history, and leave reviews and ratings for restaurants and drivers.

### 3.2. Restaurant Owner
Restaurant owners utilize the platform to manage their business operations efficiently.
- **Restaurant Management Dashboard:** A centralized, analytics-driven dashboard to monitor daily operations, total sales, and pending orders at a glance.
- **Menu Management:** Dynamically add, update, or remove menu items, categories, pricing, and food images. Toggle the availability of specific dishes in real-time.
- **Order Management:** Receive instant notifications for new orders. Accept or decline orders, and update order statuses (e.g., Order Accepted, Food is Ready) to keep customers and drivers informed.
- **Financial Analytics:** Access detailed reports on revenue, popular dishes, and overall sales performance to make informed business decisions.

### 3.3. Delivery Driver
Drivers are responsible for transporting orders from restaurants to customers.
- **Order Fulfillment:** Receive delivery requests for nearby orders. View pickup and drop-off locations before accepting a delivery.
- **Status Updates:** Update the delivery status at each stage (e.g., Picked up from Restaurant, On the Way, Delivered successfully).
- **Location Tracking:** Share real-time GPS coordinates via their mobile device so customers and the system can track the delivery progress accurately.
- **Earnings & History:** Track daily earnings, view completed delivery history, and manage payout methods.

### 3.4. Admin
Administrators have full oversight and control over the entire platform to ensure smooth operations.
- **User Management:** Monitor, suspend, or manage accounts for Customers, Restaurant Owners, and Delivery Drivers. 
- **Restaurant Approvals:** Review and approve or reject new restaurant registration requests to ensure platform quality.
- **System Monitoring:** Oversee all active orders, intervene in case of disputes, and resolve support tickets or complaints.
- **Financial Oversight:** Manage commission rates, process payouts to restaurants and drivers, and analyze platform-wide revenue and growth metrics.

## 4. Core Features Requirements
This project strictly adheres to the requested core capabilities:
- **Order Placement:** A seamless user journey from browsing menus to placing customized orders.
- **Delivery Tracking:** Implementation of real-time GPS tracking for transparent and reliable delivery logistics.
- **Payment Integration:** Secure, multi-method online payment processing.
- **Restaurant Management Dashboard:** A comprehensive, data-rich interface empowering restaurant owners to handle menus, orders, and sales analytics effectively.
